Which findings are real?

Six hypothesis tests on the full 2008 to 2024 data, with p-values adjusted together (Holm-Bonferroni) because running several tests makes a false positive more likely. These results do not change with the season filter.

Can the winner be predicted?

Models used only information known before the match (team rating, form, record, home advantage, toss, venue). They were trained on 2008 to 2022 and tested on the 2023 and 2024 seasons, which they had never seen.

Short answer: barely better than a coin flip.

On 144 test matches, every accuracy interval includes 50%. Over nine walk-forward seasons (2016 to 2024) the best fitted model reached 54.7%. Home advantage was the one steady signal, and more complex models did worse than simple ones.

About this data
  • Ball-by-ball data from Cricsheet, packaged in the Kaggle dataset "IPL Complete Dataset (2008-2024)", cleaned and merged by franchise.
  • Super overs are excluded from ball-by-ball figures. Five matches with no result are excluded from win rates. Rain-affected first innings are excluded from the scoring test but not from the charts above.
  • Small groups give wide intervals. Chepauk has only 85 matches with a result, and the prediction test has 144.
  • The full code, tests and charts are in the project notebooks: ipl_analysis.ipynb and ipl_inference_and_prediction.ipynb.
